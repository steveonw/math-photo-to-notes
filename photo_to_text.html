Batch Photo → Text, with review piles

A modified standalone version of the study-suite Photo to Text workflow. Results are automatically sorted into Good, Review, Unclear, Math Unsure, or Failed. You can override any classification manually. PDF export renders LaTeX-style math with MathJax before opening the browser print/save-to-PDF dialog. Math-uncertain items also include a readable (I think it says: …) guess beside the structured uncertainty marker. A conservative EquationWright-style LaTeX repair pass fixes common rendering artifacts before MathJax/PDF export and records what it changed.
